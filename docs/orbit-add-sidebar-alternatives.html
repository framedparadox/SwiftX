Ten Add-to-Dock sidebars

On the Orbit Layout screen the right card stocks the dock: Apps, Shortcuts, Folders, Links, Groups, and Tools. Today it is an accordion list with ticks for items already on the ring, and Add something else… at the bottom.

Each study below keeps the same canvas and the same catalog. Only the sidebar’s shape changes — how you browse, mark what’s on the dock, and place the next item.

Accordion

The shipped pattern. One category opens at a time. Ticked rows are already on the dock. The full list scrolls inside the card.

You can see. Every kind in a familiar outline, and which apps already occupy a slot.

Gives up. Comparing two categories at once. Shortcuts hide while Apps is open.

Segmented tabs

Kinds become a row of pills across the top of the card. The list under them is always one kind, never nested.

You can see. Which kind you are in, without opening and closing headers.

Gives up. A glance at how many items sit in the other kinds. You have to tap each pill.

Icon grid

Apps are tiles with the icon first. A filter row picks the kind. On-dock tiles keep a teal outline and a short status line.

You can see. Icons at a glance — useful when names look alike.

Gives up. Dense text scanning. Long names clip; folders and links look similar as tiles.

Search first

The card opens on a search field. Results mix kinds. Filter chips narrow the hit list. Browse categories sit under the results as a fallback.

You can see. Fast path when you already know the name.

Gives up. Discovery. An empty query still needs the lower browse list, or the card feels empty.

Icon rail

A thin column of kind icons stays pinned on the left edge of the card. The right pane is only the active list.

You can see. Kind switching without eating vertical space from the list.

Gives up. Labels on the kinds. New users have to learn the six glyphs.

Suggestions shelf

A horizontal shelf of recent and suggested items sits above the accordion. Most adds never open a category.

You can see. The next likely app without scrolling.

Gives up. Quiet chrome. The shelf and the accordion compete for the same short card.

Slot map

Eight cells mirror the ring. Pick an empty cell, then pick from the list below. Filled cells show what already sits there.

You can see. Exactly which place on the dock you are stocking, before you drag.

Gives up. Free drag onto a bubble. Placement becomes a two-step form.

Batch checklist

Rows are checkboxes. Items already on the dock stay ticked and locked. A footer button adds the selected set in one step, filling free slots in order.

You can see. A shopping list of what will land on the ring.

Gives up. Instant placement. Nothing appears until you confirm the batch.

Kind cards

The first screen of the sidebar is six large kind cards with counts. Opening one replaces the card with that kind’s list and a back link.

You can see. How much stock each kind holds before diving in.

Gives up. One-click access to Apps. Every add is a drill-in.

Compact rail

The sidebar collapses to a narrow icon strip so the ring keeps width. Activating a kind flies the full catalog out as an overlay panel beside the strip.

You can see. A large preview, with Add still one click away.

Gives up. A permanently visible catalog. The list covers part of the canvas while open.
